Return & Exchange Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Glowzy House, our gift baskets, bouquets and gift boxes are prepared with care for each order.

No Returns or Exchanges

Due to the nature of our products, Glowzy House generally does not accept returns or exchanges after an order has been delivered.

Incorrect or Damaged Items

If you receive an item that is different from what you ordered or significantly damaged upon delivery, please contact us as soon as possible.

Please provide:

  • Your order details
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the item, where applicable

We will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution.

Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for providing the correct delivery address, contact number, order details and recipient information.
